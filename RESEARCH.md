# Market Research — Metro Lahore Kites Catalogue

**Observed:** 2026-10-21
**Purpose:** Ground the seeded product catalogue (names, size units, variant
lists) in what Lahore kite retailers actually list, so the store uses the
vocabulary customers recognise — *tawa*, *githi*, *adda*, *pona*, *pinna*.

> **All prices below are indicative third-party observations only.**
> Metro Lahore Kites publishes **no** prices in this system: every catalogue
> entry ships as *"Price to be confirmed"*. Real prices are entered by the
> business owner in Admin → Products. The same notes are stored per-product
> in the database (`products.price_research`, visible to staff in the admin
> product editor) with this disclaimer attached.

## Observations by product

### Kites (patang & gudda)

| Size vocabulary seen on sources | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Adda / 0.5 Tawa, 0.75 Tawa (pona), 1 Tawa, 1.5 Tawa | Standard gudda size ladder |
| 4 Githi, 4.5 Githi | Common patang size marks |

Observed prices:

- **lahorekites.com** — Adda 0.5 Tawa Rs 220; 0.75 Tawa (pona) Rs 240;
  1 Tawa from Rs 350; 1.5 Tawa from Rs 475.
- **basantshop.com** — 1 Tawa kite Rs 350; 1.5 Tawa kite Rs 450;
  1.5 Tawa **premium** Rs 600.
- **kite.com.pk** — lists a patang/gudda range with per-piece prices varying
  by size and design (no single fixed figure used).

*Catalogue impact:* the gudda variant ladder is `Adda / 0.5 Tawa · 0.75 Tawa ·
1 Tawa · 1.5 Tawa`; classic patang uses `Small · Standard · 4 Githi · 4.5
Githi`; a separate premium patang carries the premium price tier concept.

### Dor / Pinna (cotton string on spool)

- **kite.com.pk** — 1 Piece Pinna ₨ 6,500; 1.5 Piece ₨ 8,500; 2 Piece ₨ 15,000.
- **basantshop.com** — Pinna, 2 piece Rs 8,000.
- **lahorekites.com** — Kite String (Dor Pinna) 2 Pc from Rs 8,999.

*Catalogue impact:* variant ladder `0.5 Piece · 1 Piece · 1.5 Piece · 2 Piece`.
The wide price spread across sources (Rs 8,000–15,000 for 2 piece) is exactly
why no price is published — the business must set its own.

### Kits

- **lahorekites.com** — a "Basant 2 Day Bulk Kite Bundle" (42 pcs, gudda &
  pinna combo) listed at Rs 38,999, used only as an indicative bundle price
  point.

*Catalogue impact:* `Small Kit · Standard Kit · Premium Kit`; contents are
confirmed with the order rather than fixed in the listing.

### Accessories

- **basantshop.com** — gloves Rs 750; medical tape Rs 200.
- **lahorekites.com** — hand protection tape Rs 300; premium kite packing
  bag from Rs 750.

*Catalogue impact:* gloves, hand-protection tape and carrying bags seeded as
accessories, all price-TBC.

## Explicit non-findings

- **No Basant 2027 dates are claimed anywhere in the product.** Publicly
  circulating date lists for the 2027 season conflicted between sources, and
  permitted flying days are set by the authorities each season — the UI
  tells customers to confirm the rules that apply to their days instead.
- **No coated strings.** Sources list coated/manja products; this catalogue
  deliberately stocks **cotton dor/pinna only**, and the configurable legal
  notice states that glass-coated, chemical-coated, metal-coated and nylon
  cutting strings are not sold.
- **No invented WhatsApp number.** Several sources publish WhatsApp numbers
  for their own shops; nothing comparable was configured for Metro Lahore
  Kites — the storefront's WhatsApp button stays hidden until the business
  enters its own number in Admin → Settings.

## Caveats

1. Prices above were observed on **2026-10-21** and change constantly with
   the season; treat them as calibration hints, not quotes.
2. Nothing here implies any relationship with the cited retailers. URLs are
   cited as research references only.
3. Size ladders (tawa/githi/piece) are folk units that vary between
   manufacturers; final dimensions are confirmed per order — the catalogue
   says so on every kite listing.
4. Kite-flying regulations in Punjab change every season; the legal notice
   text in Admin → Settings is the place to re-check and update the published
   guidance before each season.
